# M0 + M1 status: 2026-11-14

## What exists now

**M0 (networking)**
- `NetBootstrap` handles host/join over two backends: **Direct** (Unity Transport, port 7777: same PC, LAN, Tailscale) and **Steam** (AppID 480 relay via Facepunch.Steamworks 2.5.2).
- `SteamTransport` is a custom Netcode transport over Steam Networking Sockets. `SteamLobby` handles host, "Find hunts" browser (filtered to our game and protocol version), join, and overlay invites.
- Owner-authoritative first-person movement, plus host-simulated physics boxes.

**M1 ("One Rabbit" slice)**
- **Forest test map** (`HuntTest`), 160×160 m: 90 trees, rocks, fog, invisible edge walls, baked NavMesh.
- **Camp:** trader NPC with a yellow **sell pad**. Drop or throw a carcass on it to sell it for the team.
- **Rabbits:** the host runs the AI. They wander and graze, bolt from players within 9 m and from gunshots within 30 m, zig-zag while fleeing, and hop when moving. 8 are kept alive and respawn every 6 s.
- **Rifle:** left click shoots (hitscan, 0.7 s bolt cycle), right click aims. A body shot does 60 damage (a rabbit has 50 HP), a headshot always kills.
- **Kills** launch the rabbit up and away, spinning. The carcass becomes a physics object.
- **Trick shots** (shown in the toast and the sale line):
  - Headshot ×1.5
  - Long shot (≥35 m) ×1.25
  - Jump shot (you're airborne) ×1.25
  - **Juggle**: each extra shot on an *airborne* carcass adds +0.5×, up to 6. Shooting a grounded carcass pops it back into the air for another try.
- **Carry:** `E` picks up or drops, left click throws while carrying, and you move 20% slower. Network ownership passes to the carrier so it doesn't lag on their screen.
- **Economy:** shared team money (HUD top right), a sale toast feed, and an interaction hint at the bottom center.
  - Price = $12 base × (weight ÷ 2.25 kg) × style multiplier.

**Tooling**
- `FriendSlop ▸ Build Hunt Test Scene` regenerates the map, prefabs, materials and navmesh from code.
- `FriendSlop ▸ Build Windows Zip` creates `Builds/FriendSlopHunting-Windows-<stamp>.zip`, which includes `steam_appid.txt`.
- **Editor command bridge** (editor-only, dev): tools outside Unity can trigger a fixed whitelist of actions by writing to `Temp/fsh_command.txt`: build, play, host, screenshot, and host-side self-tests.
- **Build flags:**
  - `-fsh-host`
  - `-fsh-join <ip>`
  - `-fsh-quit-after <s>`
  - `-nosteam`

## Game feel & HUD polish pass (2026-11-15)
Research first. Subtitles only, no video played:
- GMTK, "Secrets of Game Feel and Juice"
- GMTK, "The Power of Video Game HUDs"
- "A Guide to Good Combat Feel" (a breakdown of Valheim's combat)
- A Giovanni Goes to Pizza devlog on FPS feel
- Lexispell's UI tween tips
- "5 UI Principles"
- "How To Fail At UI"

Takeaways:
- Layer feedback on every hit.
- Use Perlin-noise shake, never random jitter.
- Animate anything on the HUD that changes, and keep it snappy.
- Put the most important info front and centre.
- Let players turn shake off.

What changed:
- **Camera**
  - Trauma-based shake on shots, explosions, tank fire and taking damage.
  - Head bob and a small roll while walking.
  - A dip and a thud when you land from a fall.
  - FOV pushes out while sprinting.
  - New options: **Screen shake** slider (0 = off) and a **Head bob** toggle.
- **Gun**
  - Recoil kicks the gun up with a random twist, then springs back.
  - The gun lags behind the mouse (sway) and bobs with your steps.
- **Hits**
  - Animals flash white and squash on every hit, for everyone.
  - The shooter gets floating damage numbers (gold for headshots) and four-stroke hit markers that pop out (gold for headshots, red on a kill).
  - On a kill: a kill sound, plus "DEER DOWN · HEADSHOT ~$86" under the crosshair.
- **Crosshair:** new animated crosshair that opens with weapon spread, movement, jumping and recent shots, and tightens when aiming.
- **HUD animation**
  - Team money counts up.
  - Toasts slide and pop in.
  - The interaction hint pops in.
  - The equipped weapon row stretches in, and the weapon name flashes when you switch.
  - The health bar shakes, and the chunk you just lost lingers before draining.
  - Being hurt shows a red edge vignette instead of a full-screen tint, and it pulses at low health.
  - Level-ups and achievements get a big centre banner with new fanfare sounds.
- **Menus:** every button plays a soft tick on hover and a click when pressed.
- Fixed the "scroll" key label overlapping "Bazooka" in the weapon list.
- Dev bridge: `shoot-zoo <animal> <dist> [head]`, `banner-test`, `hurt-test`.

## Fixes, kill feed, chat & economy v3 (2026-11-15)
Research (subtitles only):
- "I Reworked Overwatch's Killfeed System"
- "Cool Economy and Progression Mechanics in Games"
- Extra Credits, "Progression Systems"

Takeaways: a kill feed reads killer → weapon → victim, marks headshots and gives deaths personality. Prices should make buying a real decision, and progression should pace the game rather than pad it.

- **Fixed: dead animals turning white and the "flat moose".** A hit landing mid-flash restarted the flash from the half-white, half-squashed state, and that stacked up. Each animal's real colours and size are now captured once and restored before every flash. The killing hit no longer squashes, because the ragdoll takes over.
- **Fixed: clouds sinking into peaks.** Each cloud checks the highest ground under its footprint and rises to keep at least 38 m of clearance, then drifts back to its normal height.
- **Death messages:** about 40 lines.
  - General animal lines.
  - Lines for birds, moose/elk/deer, bears, pigs and canines.
  - Boss lines, skunk lines, and friendly-fire and bazooka lines that name the shooter.
  - The same line never shows twice in a row.
- **Kill feed** (top right):
  - Killer ▸ [weapon] ▸ victim.
  - ◎ marks a headshot, ↗ a long shot, ⤴ a jump shot.
  - Bosses are orange, hunter deaths show as "Moose ☠ Dapu" in red.
  - Your own kills are highlighted gold, your deaths red.
  - Everyday "X bagged a deer" toasts were removed (the feed covers them). Boss, bounty and elite kills still get a toast.
- **Team chat:**
  - **T** (rebindable, "Team chat" in Controls) or **Enter** to type. Enter sends, Esc cancels.
  - Names have a colour per player, lines fade after 12 s and all come back while typing.
  - 120-character limit and a light spam guard on the host. Players can't use formatting tags.
- **Economy v3**
  - Weapons: Revolver $250, Slingshot $150, Uzi $650, Shotgun $800, Boomerang $450, Bow $1,000, Bolt Rifle $1,500, Machine Gun $2,800, Minigun $6,000, Bazooka $9,000. Old assets are repriced automatically.
  - Upgrades cost about 2.6× more. The tank costs $7,500.
  - Kill XP is about half (style bonuses now make up more of it). Selling gives a tenth of the sale as XP instead of a quarter. Career bucks are 15% of team sales instead of 25%.
- Dev bridge: `chat <text>`, `die-test <attacker>`, `cloud-check`.
