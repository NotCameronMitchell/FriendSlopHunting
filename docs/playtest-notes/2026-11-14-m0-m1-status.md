# M0 + M1 status: 2026-11-14

Branch: `m0/networking-spike`. **Nothing is committed yet.**

## What exists now

**M0 (networking)**
- `NetBootstrap` handles host/join over two backends: **Direct** (Unity Transport, port 7777: same PC, LAN, Tailscale) and **Steam** (AppID 480 relay via Facepunch.Steamworks 2.5.2).
- `SteamTransport` is a custom Netcode transport over Steam Networking Sockets. `SteamLobby` handles host, "Find hunts" browser (filtered to our game and protocol version), join, and overlay invites.
- Owner-authoritative first-person movement, plus host-simulated physics boxes.

**M1 ("One Rabbit" slice)**
- **Forest test map** (`HuntTest`), 160×160 m: 90 trees, rocks, fog, invisible edge walls, baked NavMesh.
- **Camp:** trader NPC with a yellow **sell pad**. Drop or throw a carcass on it to sell it for the team.
- **Rabbits:** the host runs the AI. They wander and graze, bolt from players within 9 m and from gunshots within 30 m, zig-zag while fleeing, and hop when moving. 8 are kept alive and respawn every 6 s.
- **Rifle:** left click shoots (hitscan, 0.7 s bolt cycle), right click aims. A body shot does 60 damage (a rabbit has 50 HP), a headshot always kills.
- **Kills** launch the rabbit up and away, spinning. The carcass becomes a physics object.
- **Trick shots** (shown in the toast and the sale line):
  - Headshot ×1.5
  - Long shot (≥35 m) ×1.25
  - Jump shot (you're airborne) ×1.25
  - **Juggle**: each extra shot on an *airborne* carcass adds +0.5×, up to 6. Shooting a grounded carcass pops it back into the air for another try.
- **Carry:** `E` picks up or drops, left click throws while carrying, and you move 20% slower. Network ownership passes to the carrier so it doesn't lag on their screen.
- **Economy:** shared team money (HUD top right), a sale toast feed, and an interaction hint at the bottom center.
  - Price = $12 base × (weight ÷ 2.25 kg) × style multiplier.

**Tooling**
- `FriendSlop ▸ Build Hunt Test Scene` regenerates the map, prefabs, materials and navmesh from code.
- `FriendSlop ▸ Build Windows Zip` creates `Builds/FriendSlopHunting-Windows-<stamp>.zip`, which includes `steam_appid.txt`.
- **Editor command bridge** (editor-only, dev): tools outside Unity can trigger a fixed whitelist of actions by writing to `Temp/fsh_command.txt`: build, play, host, screenshot, and host-side self-tests.
- **Build flags:**
  - `-fsh-host`
  - `-fsh-join <ip>`
  - `-fsh-quit-after <s>`
  - `-nosteam`

## How to test
1. In Unity: **FriendSlop ▸ Build Hunt Test Scene** (already run once; re-run any time). Press **Play**, then **Host**.
2. Hunt: find a rabbit in the trees, shoot it, press `E` to pick it up, carry it to the yellow pad by the blue trader, and press `E` to drop it (or left click to throw).
3. Try a juggle: shoot a rabbit, then shoot the carcass again while it's in the air.
4. Second player: **Window ▸ Multiplayer ▸ Multiplayer Play Mode**, tick Player 2, press Play. Host in one window, **Join 127.0.0.1** in the other.
5. Steam: **FriendSlop ▸ Build Windows Zip** and send it to a friend. You click Host on Steam; they click Find hunts → Join.

## M2a: weapons + shop (added later on 2026-11-14)
- **11 weapons**, unlimited ammo. Keys 1–9 and 0 pick **your inventory slots** in order, and the scroll wheel cycles.
  - Pistol (free starter)
  - Revolver $60
  - Slingshot $40
  - Uzi $150
  - Shotgun $180 (8 pellets)
  - Boomerang $120 (comes back, hits both ways)
  - Bow $220 (silent: doesn't spook animals)
  - Bolt Rifle $300 (scope zoom)
  - Machine Gun $500
  - Minigun $1,000 (spin-up)
  - Bazooka $1,500 (explosion)
- **Gun Shop:** a hut west of camp with a gunsmith behind the counter and a **wall rack showing every weapon and its price**. Look at the gunsmith and press **E**.
  - The **trader** (east, yellow pad) only buys carcasses.
- **Personal inventory:** buying spends the *team's* money but the gun goes into **your** inventory, and the buyer auto-equips it.
  - **Tab** opens the inventory: slot keys, stats, Equip and Drop.
  - The **pistol is permanent** and can't be dropped.
- **Dropping and giving:** **G** (or Drop) drops the equipped weapon in front of you.
  - Dropped guns are **real physics objects**: they lie flat on the ground, have a hitbox fitted to each gun's shape, and can be kicked, shot or blown around.
  - Anyone can press **E** on a dropped gun to take it, unless they already have that weapon. This is how you give a gun to a friend.
  - A player who leaves drops all their guns where they stood.
- Weapon stats live in `Assets/_Game/Data/Weapons/*.asset`, so you can tune them in the Inspector. Rebuilding the scene keeps your tuning.
- **Editor-only cheat:** **F9 = +$1,000** for testing the shop. It doesn't work in builds.
- Verified automatically:
  - Buying all 10 through the real host RPC ($5,000 → $930), and auto-equip.
  - Every weapon fires with no errors (hitscan, pellets, projectiles, boomerang, rocket), and the explosion kills multiple rabbits.
  - **Drop → pickup round trip:** the inventory goes 11 → 10 → 11 slots, you fall back to the pistol, and the weapon re-equips.
  - Three dropped guns came to rest on the ground with their physics asleep and per-weapon hitboxes (Minigun 0.35×0.22×1.01 m, Machine Gun 0.25×0.14×1.32 m, Bazooka 0.26×0.14×0.90 m).
  - **Shooting a dropped gun through the real aim-and-fire path moves it** (measured from 4 m): Pistol 1.1–1.3 m, Shotgun 4.9 m, Bolt Rifle 4.6–4.9 m, Bow 1.9 m, Slingshot 2.3 m, Boomerang 0.4 m. Hits on objects play a metallic tink.
  - Fixed along the way: arrows and rocks used to clip the ground just in front of low objects. Projectiles now use the exact center line against ground and walls, and the forgiving sphere only against targets.
- Not verified: how each weapon *feels*, prices and economy pace, boomerang catching, and **handing a gun to a real second player** (drop/pickup has only been tested by the same player). Remote players seeing projectiles over Steam is also untested.
- Deferred: the **drivable tank** (needs a vehicle system).

## M2b: mobs and bosses (2026-11-14, late)
- **Player health:** 100 HP. Animal hits knock you back, flash the screen red, and shake the camera.
  - At 0 HP you're **down**: you tip over, drop what you carry, and see "YOU GOT WRECKED".
  - **You respawn at camp after 5 s**, with your inventory kept and 3 s of spawn protection. Getting downed is announced to everyone.
- **8 species**, each a settings asset in `Assets/_Game/Data/Animals/` (stats tunable in the Inspector), with procedural models and fitted hitboxes:

  | Animal | Temperament | Notes | Base $ |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Rabbit | skittish | hops | 12 |
  | Duck | skittish | pairs, quacks | 8 |
  | Goose | **aggressive** | chases and pecks (4 dmg), honks, pairs | 20 |
  | Deer | skittish | fast, antlers, 50–90 kg | 45 |
  | Boar | **aggressive** | charges for 18 dmg, tusks | 35 |
  | Wolf | **predator** | **packs of 3** that aggro together, 12 dmg | 40 |
  | Moose | **territorial** | 450 HP, 30 dmg, 350–500 kg | 120 |
  | Bear | **predator** | 600 HP, 35 dmg | 200 |
- **Fighters** charge whoever shoots them. Some bail out when badly hurt, and they ignore gunshots instead of fleeing. Predators keep hunting the next nearest player.
- **Boss: Big Doug the Elk.** A giant elk with 2,500 HP and 40 damage.
  - Summon him at the **stone totem 36 m south of camp** for **$250** of team money.
  - A **boss health bar** shows while he's alive. Killing him pays an **$800 bounty** instantly, and the carcass sells too.
- **Carry speed scales with weight:** a rabbit barely slows you, a moose is a slog.
- **Camp safe zone** (24 m around camp):
  - Fighters don't spawn within it plus 16 m, won't aggro players at camp, give up a chase when you get back, and walk out if they wander in. Skittish animals can still roam near camp.
  - Verified: two angry geese → 0 angry within 4 s of the player reaching camp, and no more damage taken.
  - The long-term plan (difficulty rings on the bigger map) is in `docs/ideas.md`.
- **Fixed (playtest report):** 3D text (gun shop sign, rack tags, trader sign) showed through walls and mirrored from behind. It now uses a custom `FriendSlop/WorldText` shader that respects depth and is one-sided. The gun shop sign was raised above the roof edge.
- **Synthesized creature voices** (quack, honk, grunt, growl, bellow, roar) for idle calls, aggro, attacks, getting hurt and death.
- **Verified automatically:**
  - All 8 species spawn at their populations.
  - Damage, down and respawn work.
  - A goose pair aggroed together and attacked the player.
  - Boss summon (−$250) → bounty (+$800) works, and the boss bar shows.
- **Balance notes:** two geese originally downed a player in about 10 s, so goose damage was cut from 6 every 0.9 s to 4 every 1.2 s. Predators near the map edge are dangerous, which is why the totem moved closer to camp.
- **Not verified:** how fights *feel*, wolf pack behavior with real players, shooting a charging boar or bear, and big carcasses carried over Steam.

## Known rough edges
- The UI is placeholder IMGUI. Rabbits and players are primitives.
- No sounds yet. The gunshot is silent, which is the biggest feel gap.
- Shots are client-claimed with host sanity checks (3 m tolerance). Fine for friends, not cheat-proof (by design).
- Killing as a client shows "Player N" in toasts. Steam names come later.
- `FriendSlop ▸ Build Windows Zip` opens an Explorer window when it finishes.
